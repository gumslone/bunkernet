BunkerNet

Local network, no internet needed. Chat, private messages, shared data and emergency alerts that work when the internet does not.

1

Join the Wi-Fi

Scan the first code, or open your phone's Wi-Fi settings and choose BunkerNet. (no password)

2

Open the app

A page opens by itself. If not, scan the second code or type 192.168.4.1 in your normal browser (Chrome, Safari). If a small pop-up window keeps forgetting your name, use the normal browser instead.

3

Choose a name

Type a name when asked. Tap your name at the top right to change it later.

How to use it

Chat tab"Everyone" is the group chat. Tap a person's name to send them a private message.
✓ ✓✓ ⏳✓ sent. ✓✓ delivered. ⏳ stored: it is delivered the moment that person is back on the network.
🚨 buttonRaises a loud red alert on every device. Only for real emergencies. Anyone can clear it.
Data tabShared notes and values for everyone, such as water level or fuel.
Nodes tabShows which relay boxes are working and how far away they are.

Good to know

  • There is no internet. Only people on this network can see your messages.
  • Messages are short text, up to about 176 letters.
  • Times come from each person's own phone clock.
  • Relay boxes keep messages for offline people while they have power.
